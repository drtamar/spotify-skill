# Funky Day Party Journey
**12-Hour Multi-Genre Day Party Playlist**
**Vibe:** Groovy, funky, ass-moving, tribal → dancehall/reggaeton/funk → peak energy → sunset wind down
**Goal:** Keep people moving without getting stuck in one genre. Day party energy — sunny, sexy, not dark night-rave aggression.

---

## Phase 1: Warm-Up (First 2–2.5 hours)
**Goal:** People arriving, chilling, getting comfortable. Tribal, organic, percussion-heavy, groovy, slightly psychedelic. Didgeridoo/percussion friendly.

**Suggested tracks (add in this order):**

- Ganga Giri – Didjeridoo (or any strong didgeridoo + percussion track)
- Ash Dargan – Spirit of the Earth
- TJ Rehmi – One
- Quantic Soul Orchestra – Pushin' On
- St Germain – Rose Rouge
- Thievery Corporation – Lebanese Blonde
- Bonobo – Kerala
- Emapea – Mind
- Nickodemus – Sun People (feat. The Real Live Show)
- Fat Freddy's Drop – Roady
- Khruangbin – Time (You and I)
- The Cinematic Orchestra – To Build a Home (early, very chill)
- Lane 8 – Road
- Desert Dwellers – Wandering Sadhu
- Porangui – Medicina

**Total for Phase 1:** ~15–18 tracks (roughly 90–110 min)

## How to Use This Playlist

1. Create a new Spotify playlist (you already have one).
2. Go through each phase in order.
3. Search for the songs and add them.
4. For better flow, put **~3–5 songs per phase** first as a test, then expand.
5. Use **Crossfade** (Settings → Playback → Crossfade) set to 6–10 seconds for smooth transitions.
6. Optional: Create 4 separate playlists (one per phase) so you can manually jump between sections if needed.
